Day 1

Pass the Apple Ads keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the keyword each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per search

Your name, the feels-like searches and the hazard searches each get their own first screens and paywall; every other keyword keeps today’s first launch.

Week 2

Judge each search on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on trials, paid subscriptions and what each install from that keyword earns, so bids follow the search that pays.

We found no active Meta ads for WeatherBug in the Meta Ad Library, and your last YouTube creative launched on 9 August 2026. On Apple Ads you hold 38% of US ad slots on your own name “weatherbug” (83% on “weatherbug – weather forecast”), so other apps take most of the slots above your own listing: a brand defence worth checking. Some terms bring people who aren’t looking for weather: “moen smart water network” (57%), “tan time starts now”, “conditionsofuse”, “conditions.html”, “ways”, “whether”. Worth checking what those installs earn. The search data is account-level, so it can’t tell WeatherBug from WeatherBug Elite. This layer carries no range: only a test per search can give one.
